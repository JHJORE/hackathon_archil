## Design Context

Audience: a procurement operator reviewing RFQs, discovering suppliers, and monitoring reviewed email negotiations. This is a local hackathon demo with live research and durable Archil records.

Workflow: review an RFQ from Scout or enter one manually; complete missing specifications and commercial bounds; run supplier research; inspect evidence; approve individual outreach and counteroffer messages; monitor replies.

Confirmed visual tone: calm industrial — light, precise, confident. Favor a warm neutral canvas, restrained copper actions and green status accents, strong typographic hierarchy, flat operational tables and generous space around decisions. Keep email reviews and unresolved source requirements prominent. Machine observability is available through progressive disclosure.

Architecture: one operator dashboard over separate rfq-scout (read-only input) and out-reach (case and negotiation ownership) disks. Imported source revisions retain hashes and original records. No automatic sends or disk migration.

Accessibility: clear keyboard focus, labeled controls, responsive layouts, reduced motion, readable contrast. Distinguish real research, saved evidence and simulated/test negotiations explicitly.
